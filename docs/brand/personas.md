# Echo Theory Labs — Personas

Read by `draft-content` (to target the right reader) and `brand-review` (to flag persona-mismatched copy).

---

## Persona 1: The Production-AI CTO (primary)

A CTO or head of AI engineering at a mid-to-large org running autonomous AI systems in production. Has shipped at least one agentic system and felt the failure modes — silent degradation, runaway cost, prompt injection scares, integrations that drift after a model upgrade. Owns the budget for fixing it. Reads research-grade content because they need to make defensible architectural calls in front of a board that has seen the Gartner numbers. Distrusts vendor marketing, trusts methodology and evidence. Address them as a peer making consequential decisions on incomplete information; never as a buyer to be converted.

## Persona 2: The Platform-Lead Staff Engineer (secondary)

A staff or principal engineer running a platform, infrastructure, or AI-engineering team. Influences the CTO's tooling and methodology choices. Reads to refine their own mental model and to bring credible references back to their leadership. Expects deep technical specificity — exact failure modes, named patterns, citations. Cares about whether a methodology survives contact with their existing stack. Address them as a fellow practitioner; never as an audience to be entertained or impressed.
