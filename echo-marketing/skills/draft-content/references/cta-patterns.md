# Call-to-Action Patterns (Echo Theory Labs)

Echo distributes research, not funnels. Every CTA in Echo content is a research-distribution CTA. Lead-gen CTAs are explicitly forbidden by `brand/anti-patterns.md` and have no place in any Echo channel.

## Principles

- **Research-distribution framing**: a CTA points the reader to a deeper artifact (a methodology note, an audit checklist, a longer post, a published case study), not to a sales process.
- **Declarative, not promotional**: "Full methodology:" beats "Learn more about how we can help you!"
- **One CTA per piece** in long-form (blog, methodology note); zero or one in Twitter/X (only on the closing tweet of a thread).
- **No urgency manufactured**: Echo never invents scarcity ("limited spots", "today only", "while it lasts"). If urgency is real (a published deadline, an embargo), state the date plainly.
- **No risk-reversal idioms**: "no credit card required", "cancel anytime", "free for 14 days" — all forbidden. They collapse Echo into a SaaS frame.

## Echo CTA forms (use these)

- **Pointer to a deeper artifact**: "Full methodology and citations:" then the link.
- **Pointer to a companion post**: "We cover the audit checklist in detail here:" then the link.
- **Pointer to a published case study**: "How we ran this on our own infrastructure:" then the link.
- **Pointer to a published practice-area post**: "On the broader discipline of Agent Harness Engineering:" then the link.
- **Open invitation to engage on substance**: "If your team is running into this, we are publishing more on it — [link]." (Acceptable; soft and substance-led.)

## Forbidden CTA forms (do not use)

- "Start your free trial."
- "Book a demo."
- "Sign up to learn more."
- "No credit card required."
- "Limited spots available — book today."
- "Get in touch to see how we can help you."
- Any "click here" or "read more" link text — link text must be descriptive (per `brand/style-guide.md`).

## Channel-specific notes

- **Blog post**: end with a single research-distribution CTA. In-line CTAs only when contextually load-bearing (e.g., a related-methodology mention mid-post). The conclusion is the right place for the primary CTA.
- **Twitter/X**: optional CTA only on the closing tweet of a thread. Single-tweet posts may omit the CTA entirely; the body of the tweet is the value.

## Replacement examples (lead-gen → Echo research-distribution)

| Lead-gen (forbidden) | Echo replacement |
|---|---|
| "Start your free trial of our trajectory analysis platform." | "Full trajectory analysis methodology, with worked examples: [link]." |
| "Book a demo to see drift detection in action." | "How we run drift detection on our own production agents: [link]." |
| "Get in touch to learn how we can help your team." | "If your team is running into this, our published methodology notes are the best starting point: [link]." |
