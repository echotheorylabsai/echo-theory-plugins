# Channel: Instagram Post

## Structure

- **Hook** — first line must work within the caption-preview crop.
- **Body** — storytelling-driven, line breaks for readability.
- **CTA** — "link in bio", comment prompt, save/share prompt.
- **Hashtags** — 3–10 relevant tags. Place in the first comment or as a tight block at the end of the caption.

## Writing practices

- Visual-first: the caption supports the image/video, does not replace it.
- Line breaks between short paragraphs — Instagram's rendering collapses whitespace without them.
- Emojis are expected and context-appropriate.
- Stories and Reels have their own conventions; this file covers feed captions.

## When drafting

- Pair with `headline-patterns.md` for the opening hook.
