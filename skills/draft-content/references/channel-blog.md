# Channel: Blog Post (Echo Theory Labs)

Echo's blog is a research-distribution channel, not a marketing blog. The default register is closer to a methodology note or applied research paper than to a SaaS company post. Treat the reader as a peer making consequential architectural decisions.

## Structure

1. **Headline** — declarative or contrarian, names a practice area or a failure mode. Includes the primary keyword. ≤60 characters for SEO.
2. **Lead paragraph** (100–150 words) — opens with a load-bearing claim, statistic with citation, or named failure mode. State what the post will establish. Include the primary keyword. Never open with a fake-personal story.
3. **Body sections** (3–6) — descriptive H2 subheadings. H3 for sub-sections. One core idea per section, supported by evidence (cited stat, internal data with explicit "in our testing" qualifier, or worked example).
4. **Conclusion** (75–125 words) — summarize the load-bearing claims, name the practice area or methodology, end with a research-distribution CTA (link to deeper methodology, link to published companion piece). No lead-gen.
5. **Meta description** — ≤160 characters, includes primary keyword, declarative not promotional.

## Citation expectations

- Every load-bearing statistic carries an inline citation in the form `([Publisher, Month YYYY](url))`. No naked numbers.
- Internal claims unsupported by external data carry an explicit "in our testing" or "in our engagements" qualifier.
- Never paraphrase a number from memory. If the source is not at hand, cut the number or add a `[citation needed]` placeholder for the user to resolve before publishing.
- Never fabricate a source name. If unsure whether a study exists, ask the user before including it.

## Voice and tone

- Echo voice attributes (declarative, evidence-led, technical-peer) apply per `brand/voice.md` §2.
- Coin or use Echo-named practice areas where appropriate (*Applied Agentics*, *Agent Harness Engineering*, *Evaluation Engineering*, *Adversarial Defense*) per `brand/style-guide.md`.
- No marketing-blog idioms (no "imagine if...", no "let's dive in", no "the secret to...", no "5 things you didn't know about...").
- All anti-patterns from `brand/anti-patterns.md` are forbidden.

## Length norms

- **Methodology / framework post**: 900–1,500 words.
- **Practice-area naming post**: 1,000–1,500 words (more space to define the term and ground it in evidence).
- **Field-update / commentary**: 600–900 words.
- Aim for the lower end when in doubt; padding undermines authority.

## Reading level and pacing

- Technical-peer reading level. Do not paraphrase precise terms into accessible alternatives — use the precise term.
- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). Subheadings every 250–350 words.
- Active voice by default.
- Front-load the load-bearing claim of each section in its first sentence.

## When drafting

- Pair with `headline-patterns.md` for 2–3 declarative or contrarian headline options.
- Pair with `seo-patterns.md` for keyword placement (headline, first paragraph, meta description, one H2).
- Pair with `cta-patterns.md` for the conclusion CTA (research-distribution only).
