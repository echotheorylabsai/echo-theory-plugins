# Call-to-Action Patterns

## Principles

- **Action verbs**: Get, Start, Download, Join, Try, See.
- **Be specific about what happens next**: "Start your free trial" beats "Submit".
- **Create urgency only when genuine**: "Join 500 teams already using this" or "Limited spots available".
- **Reduce perceived risk**: "No credit card required", "Cancel anytime", "Free for 14 days".
- **One primary CTA** per page or email — additional choices reduce conversion.

## Examples by context

- **Blog post**: "Read our complete guide to [topic]" / "Subscribe for weekly insights"
- **Email**: "Read the full story" / "Claim your spot" / "Reply and tell us"
- **Social post**: "Drop a comment if you agree" / "Save this for later" / "Link in bio"
- **Landing page**: "Start your free trial" / "Book a demo" / "See it in action"

## Placement

- **Landing pages**: primary CTA above the fold; repeat at logical break points and at the bottom.
- **Email**: CTA appears after value is established (not in the first sentence).
- **Blog post**: end with the primary CTA; in-line CTAs only where contextually relevant (e.g., a related-guide mention).
- **Long-form pages**: repeat the primary CTA at the bottom.
