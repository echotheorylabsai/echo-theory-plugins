# Channel: Facebook Post

## Structure

- **Hook** — conversational opener, often a question.
- **Body** — short, personal, scannable.
- **CTA** — engagement prompt (comment, react, share).
- **Hashtags** — 1–3 only; Facebook rewards hashtags less than other platforms.

## Writing practices

- Conversational tone; Facebook audiences skew toward personal/community content over business updates.
- Questions drive comments.
- For posts with links, shorter posts (<80 characters) tend to get more engagement.
- Native video and images outperform link posts.
