# Channel: Twitter/X Post

## Structure

- **Hook** — first words carry the weight. Lead with a number, question, or contrarian claim.
- **Body** — concise, punchy. Single post ≤280 characters. For longer narratives, use threads.
- **CTA** — optional. Reply prompts, save/bookmark prompts, or link to a full piece.
- **Hashtags** — 0–2 relevant tags. Twitter/X rewards conversation more than discoverability.

## Writing practices

- Every character earns its place. Cut adjectives and adverbs first.
- Threads: number them (1/, 2/…) or signal continuation. First tweet must stand alone as a hook.
- Engage with replies — the algorithm rewards conversation velocity in the first hour.

## When drafting

- Pair with `headline-patterns.md` for the opening tweet of a thread or a standalone hook.
