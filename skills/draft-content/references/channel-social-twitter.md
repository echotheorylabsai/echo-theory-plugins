# Channel: Twitter/X Post (Echo Theory Labs)

Echo participates in AI R&D community discourse on Twitter/X. The register is technical-peer, not broadcast-marketing. The reader is another engineer or research lead, not a prospect.

## Structure

- **Hook (lead tweet)** — first words carry the weight. Lead with a statistic (cited where space allows), a named failure mode, a contrarian-by-data claim, or a named practice area. Never open with marketing punchiness or generic "5 things you should know" framing.
- **Body** — concise, declarative, evidence-led. Single post ≤280 characters. For longer arguments, use threads.
- **CTA** (optional, only on the closing tweet) — link to a deeper Echo blog or methodology note. Research-distribution only; never lead-gen ("free trial", "book a demo", etc. are forbidden).
- **Hashtags** — 0–1 tag. The AI R&D community on Twitter/X rewards substance over discoverability tactics.

## Link-to-blog convention

When a thread or post links to a longer Echo piece:
- The link goes in the **final tweet** of a thread, or as the closing line of a single post.
- The final-tweet line reads as a research-distribution offer, not a CTA: e.g., "Full methodology and citations:" then the link.
- Never put the link in the lead tweet — it suppresses reach and reads as broadcast.
- Never use a tracking-shortened URL when the original is reasonable length.

## Thread numbering

- Number every tweet of a thread: `1/`, `2/`, ... and `n/n` on the final tweet.
- The lead tweet must stand alone as a hook — a reader who sees only `1/` should still get a load-bearing claim.
- Each subsequent tweet should advance the argument by one step; do not pad with restatements.

## Writing practices

- Every character earns its place. Cut adjectives and adverbs first.
- Cite where space allows: `(Gartner, 2025)` is enough inline; the full link can live in the closing tweet.
- No exclamation marks. No "we're excited to..." energy. No "🚀" or marketing emoji.
- Do not subtweet or name competitors disparagingly.

## When drafting

- Pair with `headline-patterns.md` for the lead tweet (treat it as a headline).
- For multi-tweet threads, draft the lead tweet first; if it cannot stand alone as a load-bearing claim, the whole thread needs rework.
