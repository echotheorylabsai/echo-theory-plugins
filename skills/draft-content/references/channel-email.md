# Channel: Email Newsletter

## Structure

- **Subject line** — ≤50 characters. Creates curiosity or states clear value. Offer 2–3 options.
- **Preview text** — complements the subject line, does not repeat it.
- **Header/hero** — visual anchor and one-line value statement.
- **Body sections** — 2–3 content blocks. Each scannable with a bold intro sentence.
- **Primary CTA** — one clear action per email.
- **Footer** — unsubscribe link, company info, social links.

## Writing practices

- Subject lines create urgency, curiosity, or state clear value — pick one, not all three.
- Personalize where possible (name, company, behavior) but not performatively.
- One primary CTA per email — visually distinct, repeated at most once.
- Scannable body: bold key phrases, short paragraphs, bullet points.
- Test subject lines, send times, CTA copy, and layout when volume allows.
- Mobile-first — most email is read on mobile; test rendering there first.

## When drafting

- Always provide 2–3 subject line options with brief rationale (curiosity vs. value vs. urgency).
- Pair with `cta-patterns.md` for primary CTA copy.
