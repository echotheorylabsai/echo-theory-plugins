# SEO Patterns for Content (Echo Theory Labs)

Apply to web-facing Echo content (blog posts, methodology notes, anything indexed by search). Echo's SEO strategy is built around naming practice areas before the industry searches for them — keywords are an outcome of category-defining content, not the input.

## Echo keyword strategy

Two layers of keyword:

1. **Echo-named terms** (category-defining): *Applied Agentics*, *Agent Harness Engineering*, *Evaluation Engineering*, *Adversarial Defense*, *Bare-Metal Orchestration*, *Observability & Runtime*, *Generative UI*. These are low-volume today; Echo is building the search demand for them deliberately. Use the full term in headlines and first paragraphs of posts that establish or reference the practice area.
2. **Industry-current terms**: *agent evaluation*, *prompt injection*, *LLM observability*, *MCP security*, *agent reliability*, *trajectory analysis*, *drift detection*. Higher search volume; Echo competes on depth and citation rigor, not keyword density.

Default rule: **one Echo-named primary term + 2–3 industry-current secondary terms per post.** This pattern compounds — every post on a practice area lifts the named term's authority while capturing existing search demand for the adjacent industry terms.

## Keyword placement

- **Primary keyword** (the practice area or industry term most central to the post) appears in: headline, first paragraph, meta description, URL slug, at least one H2.
- **Secondary keywords** appear naturally in body copy and subheadings. Do not force them.
- Do not keyword-stuff. Echo's audience reads for substance; stuffed copy degrades authority faster than it lifts ranking.

## On-page checklist

- **Title tag** — ≤60 characters, includes the primary keyword.
- **Meta description** — ≤160 characters, includes primary keyword, declarative not promotional.
- **URL slug** — short, descriptive, includes primary keyword. Use kebab-case.
- **H1** — one per page, matches or closely reflects the title tag.
- **H2 / H3** — descriptive, include secondary keywords where natural.
- **Image alt text** — descriptive; include keyword where it fits the image, never as a stuffing target.
- **Internal links** — 2–3 links to related Echo posts, especially other entries in the same practice area.
- **External links** — 1–2 links to authoritative third-party sources (Gartner, Deloitte, LangChain, peer-reviewed research, primary documentation). Match Echo's citation rigor.

## Content-SEO integration

- Comprehensive topic coverage; depth is rewarded, especially on practice-area definitional posts.
- Answer the related questions a technical reader would ask; structure for featured snippets where applicable (definition paragraphs, numbered lists, comparison tables).
- Refresh practice-area posts on a deliberate cadence; SEO compounds from updates.
- Naming a practice area (e.g., *Agent Harness Engineering*) in a definitional post creates a long-tail keyword Echo will own as the term diffuses.
