# Channel: LinkedIn Post

## Structure

- **Hook** — first line grabs attention (question, bold statement, number).
- **Body** — 2–4 concise points or a short narrative.
- **CTA** — what the reader should do next (comment, click, share, tag).
- **Hashtags** — 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end.

## Writing practices

- Professional but human. Personal stories and lessons perform well.
- Paragraph breaks after almost every sentence for readability on mobile.
- 1,300 characters is the sweet spot before "see more" truncates the post.
- Open with a strong first line — LinkedIn cuts off around line 3 before "see more".
- Avoid outbound links in the post body; put links in the first comment to preserve reach.

## When drafting

- Pair with `headline-patterns.md` for the opening hook.
- Pair with `cta-patterns.md` for the closing prompt.
