# Brief 02 — Naming the practice: trajectory analysis as a discipline

- **Topic**: Why "trajectory analysis" deserves to be named as a discipline within Evaluation Engineering — a category-defining post that names a practice the industry is doing without naming.
- **Audience**: The Platform-Lead Staff Engineer (secondary persona from `brand/personas.md`).
- **Key messages**:
  - Teams already inspect agent traces ad-hoc when something breaks; "trajectory analysis" names the systematized version of that work.
  - The discipline includes step-level inspection, decision-point flagging, tool-call audit, and drift comparison across runs.
  - It complements (does not replace) outcome-level eval scoring; the two operate at different abstraction layers.
  - Position trajectory analysis as a sub-discipline of Echo's Evaluation Engineering pillar.
- **Length**: 1,000–1,200 words.
- **Channel**: blog
